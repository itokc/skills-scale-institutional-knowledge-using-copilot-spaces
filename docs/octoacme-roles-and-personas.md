# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Additional Cross-Functional Roles

These roles supplement the core roles above and may be combined or omitted based on project size and needs. When a role is combined or not staffed separately, assign its responsibilities to a named team member so ownership remains clear.

### Engineering Leads / Technical Leads

#### Role Summary
Engineering Leads guide the technical approach and coordinate engineering decisions and dependencies across the delivery team.

#### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Coordinate engineering estimates, technical dependencies, and implementation risks
- Promote maintainability, reliability, and alignment with agreed technical standards

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers on design, implementation, reviews, and technical risk mitigation
- Align with the Product Manager on technical trade-offs that affect scope or product outcomes
- Provide the Project Manager with estimates, dependency updates, and delivery risks
- Work with QA/Testing to plan testability and technical validation

### UX / Accessibility Designers and Researchers

#### Role Summary
UX and accessibility specialists help ensure that solutions address user needs and can be used by people with a range of abilities.

#### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and communicate findings
- Develop user flows, interaction designs, and accessibility requirements
- Help define user-centered acceptance criteria and validate proposed experiences

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to connect user needs and research findings to product requirements
- Collaborate with Developers to make designs and accessibility requirements implementable
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility validation
- Surface research, design, or review dependencies to the Project Manager

### Security and Privacy Leads

#### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise on security, privacy, and compliance risks and help teams plan appropriate safeguards.

#### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements and risks
- Recommend mitigations and coordinate security or privacy reviews
- Help define controls and validation needed before release

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advise Developers and QA/Testing on controls, remediation, and verification
- Work with the Product Manager to address requirements and product trade-offs
- Provide the Project Manager with risk-register inputs, mitigation status, and escalation needs
- Coordinate with Security on-call for security incidents, following the incident process

### Data / Analytics Leads

#### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define how project outcomes will be measured and help ensure the resulting data is useful and reliable.

#### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurement plans, event definitions, and reporting needs
- Coordinate instrumentation and data-quality checks
- Analyze results and communicate evidence about project outcomes

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to define metrics and use findings to inform product decisions
- Coordinate with Developers on instrumentation and with QA/Testing on measurement verification
- Surface measurement dependencies and readiness to the Project Manager for plans and status reporting
- Consult Security and Privacy Leads on appropriate data collection and handling

### Release / Operations Leads

#### Role Summary
Release and Operations Leads coordinate deployment readiness and operational handoff for a release.

#### Responsibilities
- Coordinate operational readiness, deployment checks, and monitoring plans
- Prepare rollback or mitigation steps and clarify operational ownership
- Support post-deployment verification and handoff to on-call support

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate release checks and deployment requirements with Developers and QA/Testing
- Align schedules, dependencies, and release risks with the Project Manager
- Partner with the Product Manager on launch timing and release readiness
- Coordinate support handoff and operational communications with Customer Support / Success Representatives

### Customer Support / Success Representatives

#### Role Summary
Customer Support and Success Representatives bring customer-impact and support-readiness needs into planning and launch.

#### Responsibilities
- Identify likely customer questions, support impacts, and readiness needs
- Prepare or coordinate help content, known-issue guidance, and feedback channels
- Share customer feedback and recurring issues with the project team

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager and Stakeholders to represent customer needs and communicate product changes
- Coordinate launch communications and operational handoff with Release / Operations Leads
- Align support readiness, dependencies, and follow-up with the Project Manager
- Provide Developers and QA/Testing with customer-reported issues and feedback that may inform fixes or validation
